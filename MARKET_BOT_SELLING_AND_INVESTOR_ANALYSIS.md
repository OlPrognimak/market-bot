# Market-Bot Selling And Investor Analysis

## Current Product Position

Market-bot can be positioned as a self-hosted market and crypto scanner with personal watchlists, user-specific alert rules, and Telegram notifications.

The strongest practical angle is not "general trading platform", because this market is crowded. A better first positioning is:

- self-hosted stock and crypto alert bot
- personal market scanner for private investors
- configurable Telegram alert system
- Spring Boot + Next.js SaaS starter for market monitoring
- multi-user watchlist and alert management tool

## Selling The Project Or Business

There are two different sale types:

1. Sell as a business.
2. Sell as source code / starter product.

If the product has users and revenue, sell it as a micro-SaaS business. If it has no paying users yet, sell it as a codebase, self-hosted product, or SaaS starter.

## Platforms To Sell

### Acquire.com

Best fit when market-bot has real users, revenue, MRR, or clear growth metrics.

Use this if the product becomes a small SaaS with subscriptions or paid self-hosted licenses.

### Flippa

General marketplace for websites, apps, SaaS products, domains, and small online businesses.

Useful if the product is early or bundled with domain, codebase, and documentation.

### Microns

Good fit for smaller micro-SaaS projects and indie products.

This may be more realistic than larger acquisition marketplaces if revenue is still small.

### SideProjectors / IndieMaker

Useful if the project is pre-revenue or mostly a side project.

This is more appropriate for selling source code, project rights, or an early-stage product.

### Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy / CodeCanyon

Good if selling market-bot as:

- source code package
- self-hosted trading alert bot
- SaaS starter template
- Docker-ready market scanner

This path does not require investors or acquisition buyers.

## Places To Find Buyers Indirectly

These platforms are better for attention and validation than direct sale:

- Product Hunt
- Indie Hackers
- Hacker News `Show HN`
- Reddit communities around micro-SaaS, trading bots, algo trading, investing, and self-hosted tools
- LinkedIn posts and direct outreach
- GitHub public demo repository or landing page

The goal is to get users, feedback, and possible buyer interest.

## Finding Investors

Investor platforms and channels:

- AngelList / Wellfound
- OpenVC
- Gust
- LinkedIn direct outreach
- local German and EU angel networks
- fintech communities
- startup accelerators
- Product Hunt launch followed by investor outreach

Investors will usually ask:

- How many active users exist?
- Is anyone paying?
- What is the exact painful problem?
- Why is this better than TradingView alerts, Finviz, Yahoo, or Telegram bot scripts?
- Is there a compliance risk because it touches financial markets?
- Can the product scale beyond personal use?

## Recommended Strategy

The best path is to first create a public, simple offer.

Recommended positioning:

> Self-hosted stock and crypto scanner with Telegram alerts, user-specific watchlists, and configurable alert thresholds.

Recommended execution:

1. Create a landing page with screenshots and a short video.
2. Prepare a Docker-based demo/install guide.
3. Add clear pricing:
   - paid self-hosted license, or
   - hosted SaaS subscription, or
   - one-time source code purchase.
4. Launch on Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, Reddit, and LinkedIn.
5. Try to get 5-10 real users.
6. Add payment through Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, or Gumroad.
7. After revenue exists, list on Acquire.com or Microns.

## Valuation Reality

Without paying users, the product is usually valued as source code or a side project. The price will likely be much lower.

With even small recurring revenue, it becomes easier to sell as a business.

Examples:

- no users, no revenue: source-code sale or small project sale
- active users, no revenue: early product with validation
- paying users / MRR: micro-SaaS acquisition candidate

## Product Improvements Before Selling

To make market-bot easier to sell or fund:

- create a clean demo mode
- add screenshots and short explainer video
- document Docker setup
- clarify Yahoo/Binance provider limitations
- add subscription/license model
- add onboarding flow
- add clear risk disclaimer that this is not financial advice
- improve UI polish on settings/dashboard
- add tests for user-specific alert behavior
- add logs/health/status page

## Best Immediate Next Step

Do not start with investors first.

First, validate demand:

1. Build a landing page.
2. Show the product publicly.
3. Get first users.
4. Try to sell a small paid license.

After that, either:

- sell the project on Microns / Acquire.com, or
- use traction to approach angels/investors.
